Four parts: an automatic check of every instrument's tuning and loudness, a check of the songs on every instrument, a sound check you listen to, and a checklist for the things only a person can judge.

1. Automatic check

Plays one note on each instrument silently (rendered offline, so you won't hear it) and measures it: tuning within 5 cents, peak level at or below -1 dBFS, no clipped samples.

InstrumentResultNoteTuningPeakRMSClipped

2. Songs check

For each instrument: every song that suits it fits (each note, chord or hit has a key, hole, button, pad or drum zone to play it on), and the start of a sample song, played silently through the instrument's own sound, is in tune (within 5 cents) and on time.

3. Sound check

Turn the volume up to a normal level. Play the calibration tone first, then each instrument, and answer each one.

4. Checklist

Try each of these in the app and tick what works. Then copy the results and send them on.
