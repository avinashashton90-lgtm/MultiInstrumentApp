# Yankee Doodle

`americanfifeopus-X30.abc` is copied unchanged from the music21 corpus (music21 10.5.0 (PyPI), corpus folder, file `miscFolk/americanfifeopus.abc`), tune X:30 of `americanfifeopus.abc`.

Licence: Public Domain (the tune is 18th century; the ABC is Jack Campin’s tidied copy of an American fife tunebook, in the music21 corpus).

`yankee.mid` is rendered from it by `tools/render-sources.py` (abc2midi 4.88).
